Breakdowns of Popular System Design Questions

542 words1 diagram

Overview: The best way to learn system design is by example. This page collects expert breakdowns of the industry's most common system design problems, written by experienced FAANG staff engineers and senior managers who have asked these questions hundreds of times in interviews. Problems that don't yet have a written breakdown can still be practiced with the AI-guided practice tool.

🧒 Layman's Explanation

Think about how a chess player gets good. They don't just play more games — they study annotated grandmaster games, where a stronger player has already worked through every move and explained why each choice was made. That's exactly what these breakdowns are: worked solutions to the questions interviewers actually ask, annotated by people who have sat on the other side of the table hundreds of times.

And just like a chess student eventually has to play their own games, reading isn't enough. Once you've absorbed the expert reasoning, you practice — even on the problems that don't have a written walkthrough yet — using the AI-guided practice tool as your sparring partner.
